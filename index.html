Before we talk

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What we do, what you get and how we work with you.

Is your service right for my business?

We work with local service businesses that need their online presence managed, with home-service trades as a core focus. We review your services, market and starting point before recommending a scope. You get a plan built around the work your business needs, not a package chosen before we understand it.

I already have a website. What would you handle?

We review your existing website alongside your profiles, listings and reviews, then identify the gaps in the overall presence. We can manage the improvements and ongoing work in your agreed scope. You do not automatically need a replacement website. We recommend the work based on the gaps we find, rather than assuming everything needs to be rebuilt.

Most of my work is referrals. What do I get from this?

We make sure your online presence backs up the reputation you have built. We manage clear service information, consistent business details and review work in your plan. You get a current, useful online presence supporting your business without having to manage it yourself. We are not asking you to replace referrals with something unproven.

Why pay you for work I could do myself?

You could manage these tasks yourself. We take on the responsibility of keeping the agreed work moving when your schedule fills up. We manage updates, review responses, listing corrections and content in your plan. You get that work handled without repeatedly trading your evenings for another marketing to-do list.

I've paid an agency before and couldn't see what they did. How are you different?

We put the scope in writing before we start and report on completed work, available visibility data and next priorities. You get concrete work to review and a clear view of what we are managing. We do not ask you to treat a vague promise of better visibility as proof that the job is getting done.

How much of my time will you need?

We need your business knowledge, accurate information, access and approvals. We manage the ongoing tasks included in your plan. You stay involved where your decisions matter; we take on the profile updates, review responses and content work instead of handing those jobs back to you.

How soon will I see a difference?

We start with the agreed priorities and report as the work progresses. We can show you completed corrections, clearer service information and other work in your scope. Search visibility and enquiries are less predictable, so we review the available data without promising a results date we cannot control.

Do you guarantee more calls or a top ranking?

No. We do not control search positions or guarantee enquiries or revenue. We commit to managing the agreed work and reporting clearly on it. You get a defined service with visible follow-through, not a ranking promise used to make a sale.

How will I know whether the work is paying off?

We report on completed work and the visibility data available to us, then explain the next priorities. We can discuss that alongside what you are seeing in real enquiries and jobs. You get a grounded picture of progress; we do not claim every new job as our result or treat a search position as the whole story.

What does it cost, and what am I committing to?

We discuss pricing on the call after understanding the scope you need. Our standard service starts with a six-month minimum commitment. We give you the services, price, payment schedule and full terms in a proposal before you decide. We also discuss the founding-client rate for our first two clients on the call.

Why do you require a six-month minimum?

We provide ongoing management, not a one-time cleanup followed by silence. We use that runway to put your plan in place, keep the work consistent and review progress. You get continuity in the work, not a guarantee of rankings, enquiries or revenue. We explain the commitment and full terms before you agree.

How do you protect my reputation when handling reviews?

We work with genuine feedback from real customers and thoughtful responses in your business voice. We use neutral invitations rather than selecting only happy customers. We do not buy reviews, reward positive ratings or block negative feedback. You get a review process based on your real work, not tactics that misrepresent it.

Can you manage this for a brand-new business?

Yes. We can build the foundation around clear service pages, accurate business information and a properly prepared profile, with the exact work set out in your plan. We discuss a staged approach where appropriate. You get help matched to your starting point instead of being treated like an established business with an established online presence.
